# Code of Conduct

We aim to keep DeepMox Support welcoming, factual, and useful.

Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Provide accurate information and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Accept that feature requests may be declined or deferred.
- Avoid harassment, threats, spam, and personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's confidential information.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Conduct concerns should be sent privately to the repository maintainer.
